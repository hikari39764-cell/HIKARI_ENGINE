HIKARI Anime V2 Combat Masks

These eight white opacity masks were generated for the HIKARI project using
OpenAI image generation at the Engine Director's request. No third-party image
or texture was supplied as an input. HIKARI then removed the generated preview
background, restored genuine alpha, isolated the atlas cells and cooked the
runtime artifacts.

The masks may be used and modified with the HIKARI project under the project's
own licensing terms. They are not represented as CC0 third-party assets.

Asset set:
- Anime V2 Slash Crescent
- Anime V2 Spin Arc
- Anime V2 Broken Ring
- Anime V2 Radial Lines
- Anime V2 Impact Crack
- Anime V2 Energy Shards
- Anime V2 Comic Smoke
- Anime V2 Vertical Cut
