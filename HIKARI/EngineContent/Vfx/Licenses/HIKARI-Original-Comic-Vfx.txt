HIKARI Original Comic Combat Masks

These seven masks are original project assets authored for HIKARI. They were
generated from deterministic geometric definitions without third-party image
or texture input. They may be used and modified with the HIKARI project under
the project's own licensing terms.

Asset set:
- Comic Burst
- Comic Slash Wedge
- Comic Quad Shard
- Comic Broken Ring
- Comic Speed Lines
- Comic Ground Crack
- Comic Smoke Puff
